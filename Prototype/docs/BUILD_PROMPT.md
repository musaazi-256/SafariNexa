Build this prototype in phases:

1. Foundations and tokens
2. Email + Google auth across customer/business/admin
3. Guest browsing and protected action context resume
4. Customer discovery/search/listings
5. Booking and payment states
6. Business portal
7. Admin portal
8. Reviews, notifications, support, safety

Do not create admin users from Google OAuth. Google identity must be checked against SafariNexa DB authorization records.
